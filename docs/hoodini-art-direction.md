# Hoodini art direction

Hoodini is a dark, mobile-first automotive interface. The selected vehicle identifies the context. On camera screens, the viewfinder takes priority. In the assistant, the conversation takes priority.

## Color and identity

Use graphite #101215 for the canvas, #1A1E23 for controls that need a separate surface, #F3F4F5 for primary text and #A7ADB4 for secondary text. Use Hoodini blue #00A3FC for the scanner corners and related blue tints for interactive accents. Keep the centered official Hoodini wordmark at its original aspect ratio.

## Vehicles and layout

Use one stationary, licensed reference photograph per model. Keep the complete vehicle legible on graphite, preserve proportions and provide author, source and license under the existing notice. Keep the vehicle name, model year and engine adjacent. Use system sans-serif type, 29–38 px page titles, 16 px body text and 24 px horizontal margins. Leave space between groups. Use thin dividers only where they identify a boundary, particularly above bottom navigation.

## Controls and navigation

Use four square blue crop corners around the primary engine scan action. Reserve the lower navigation for Inicio, Tablero and Asistente. The vehicle selector expands in place. Open the engine scanner, dashboard camera, conversational assistant and plate camera directly from their existing actions. Keep manual plate entry next to the camera and present permission or unsupported-vehicle feedback where the relevant action occurs.

## Motion

Between primary tabs, use a 155 ms ease-out fade and a 7 px vertical offset on entry. Stack screens use the platform fade. Use a 5 px offset in the opposite direction on return. Keep static controls and the car photograph still. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Use a visible keyboard focus indicator and text labels for icon-only controls.
